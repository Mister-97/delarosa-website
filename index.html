Who We Are

Thick, soft, and made from scratch.

Delarosa Sweet Treats is a Fort Worth bakery known for Big Softies — thick, chewy cookies with real flavor in every bite — and buttery Sugar Cookies that melt in your mouth. Every batch is made fresh, every time.
